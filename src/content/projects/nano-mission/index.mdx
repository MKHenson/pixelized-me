## The idea

NanoMission was a series of games that introduced children to nanotechnology, to encourage them
to study it at university. It was made in partnership with the Wellcome Trust and FEI, with
nanoscientists, artists and game designers.

The second module recreated the alveoli of a human lung. Players used nanomedicine and vesicles to
carry drugs to cancerous areas.

![NanoMission screenshot](./nano-mission.jpg)

## Delivery

The game was built with C++, Virtools and C#. The programming covered the 3D engine and the
game's authoring tools.

NanoMission was well received. It was shown over several days in town centres across the UK
Midlands.
