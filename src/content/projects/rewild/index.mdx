RE-WILD is a 3D game about time travel and natural history. It runs in the browser on a custom
WebGPU engine, with its own level editor and UI framework.

RE-WILD is one of my active projects. A dev series on YouTube tracks its progress, so follow me on my
socials to see what comes next.
