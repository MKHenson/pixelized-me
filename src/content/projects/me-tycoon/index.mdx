The game centered around young people exploring various career options and what transferable skills would be useful for that job. 
My role was primarily lead developer, but it broadened out to other areas including design, management and the odd bit of art and audio work. 
Since its conception, Metycoon has had tens of thousands of hits and has had numerous publications in various newspapers and online journals around the world.
