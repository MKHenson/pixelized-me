## The Idea
![Social Influence screenshot](./social-influence.jpg)

While at PlayGen I lead the development of the social influence platform. 
This project consists of a number of web technologies that fetch content 
from YouTube and aggregate conversations between groups of people on the subject. 
While at the company, I helped create one instance of the platform on a website for the UK government, 
but unfortunately due to NDA’s, I cannot give any more information on the project.
