## The Idea
![Twitter Conference Game screenshot 1](./twitter-game.jpg)

The more they tweeted about the conference, the more points they would win, 
and move up the game’s leader boards. The remit of the game was to get people talking 
about the various speakers or the attendees themselves. Players not only could earn points 
by tweeting about the event; the game also opened up the ability to indirectly interact with others at the 
conference without meeting them in person. Simply put – a fun new way to meet people and spread the news.
