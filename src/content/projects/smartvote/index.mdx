We are happy to share the news that Smartvote.ie has gone live and helping thousands of 
citizens better understand their election candidates and their values. 
Smartvote was created by Gordon Rose and Keith Moore and partly developed 
by Webinate's Mathew Henson & Niall Farrel.
 
Smartvote asks you 30 questions about topical issues, ranging from abortion 
to immigration, health insurance and Jobbridge. Users are then allowed to rank 
how important an issue is on a scale of one to five. Smartvote then calculates 
the candidates who best represent the user’s views, and ranks each candidate accordingly. 
Users are then shown a breakdown of how their candidates voted and who shares views closest aligned to their own. 

Cofounder Gordon Rose had this to say about the app
"Users can get up to speed on the issues and candidates in just five to ten minutes, 
about the same time it takes to read one manifesto... There is way more to understanding 
and making a respectable decision about policies than a simple yes/no statement. 
But we do believe this is a great place to start," You can read more on that interview at newstalk.com
