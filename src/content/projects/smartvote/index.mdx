## The idea

Smartvote.ie helped voters understand their election candidates. It asked 30 questions on current
issues, from abortion and immigration to health insurance and JobBridge. Users rated how much each
issue mattered to them, on a scale of one to five.

Smartvote then ranked the candidates by how closely they matched each user, and showed how each
candidate had voted.

<YouTube id="R-Qkfi8I3qM" title="Smartvote" />

Cofounder Gordon Rose described the aim:

> Users can get up to speed on the issues and candidates in just five to ten minutes, about the
> same time it takes to read one manifesto. There is way more to understanding and making a
> respectable decision about policies than a simple yes/no statement. But we do believe this is a
> great place to start.

## Delivery

Smartvote was created by Gordon Rose and Keith Moore. Webinate, with Mathew Henson and Niall
Farrel, delivered the server side: deployment, server setup, database management and the full
back-end API.

The system recorded tens of thousands of completed surveys in the run-up to the election.
