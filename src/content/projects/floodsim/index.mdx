## The idea

Flooding was becoming a recurring risk in the UK, made worse by climate change. FloodSim put
players in charge of flood policy for a set number of years. Players did not know it, but they
could not win. The point was to show that the policies governments were following at the time were
not enough.

![FloodSim screenshot](./floodsim-screenshot-1.jpg)

Advisors explained the policies under debate in government at the time. Players learned the
issues as they made their choices.

![FloodSim screenshot](./floodsim-screenshot-2.jpg)

## Delivery

FloodSim was built in ActionScript. It was a success with the public, and was featured in several
publications and on BBC News.
