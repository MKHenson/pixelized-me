## The Idea

![CHI screenshot](./climate-1.jpg)

Climate Health Impact was a game I helped develop while working at PlayGen. 
It was funded by The Welcome Trust and was made to educate people about the largely 
unknown threats that come alongside global warming – namely the increase of vectors and the spread of disease.

![CHI screenshot](./climate-2.jpg)

The game centers around how you as the player deal with the crisis of global 
warming and healthcare over time. As the planet warms, more vectors spread. Each vector 
breakout requires you to make policy descions to help limit the damage and save as many people as possible.

![CHI screenshot](./climate-3.jpg)

The game was developed using Action Script and Flash Builder. I came onto this project 
quite late in its development, but was asked to fully revamp the project on a very short deadline. 
You can play the game and find out more from the playgen page.
